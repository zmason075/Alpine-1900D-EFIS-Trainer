EFIS Failure Trainer

Each scenario sets a failure on the displays. Find it, work the reversionary panel, and the correct switch brings up the remediation and the QRH checklist.

Order
How to use
  • Swipe a switch up or down to move it, one position per swipe. You can also tap above or below the switch’s pivot, or use the arrow keys.
  • A switch outlined in orange is out of NORM / OFF. Green means it’s in the correct position.
  • When the correct switch is set, the remediation and the QRH checklist show at the bottom.
  • XDTA (cross-side data bus): no switch action is needed. Leave the panel in NORM / OFF and tap “No switch action needed.”
